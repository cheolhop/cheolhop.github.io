Cheol Park

Developing foundation models for self-driving.

I’m a staff research engineer at Waymo, where I develop foundation models for self-driving, including supervised fine-tuning (SFT) and reinforcement learning (RL) of large language models for long-tail scenarios. My work spans multimodal world models, multi-agent prediction, and planning. Earlier, at Google, I worked on machine learning models for human detection and tracking, user modeling, spam detection, and semantic search. I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science from KAIST, advised by Daeyeon Park and Kyu Ho Park.

Research interests
